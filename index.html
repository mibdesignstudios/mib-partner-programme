How it works

Four steps, and only the first one is yours

  1. 1

    Share the lead

    Send your client's name, number and property details using the form below. Your lead is registered to you for 90 days.

  2. 2

    We take it from there

    We call within 24 hours, visit the site, and present a design concept and quotation. We keep you posted at each stage.

  3. 3

    Client signs

    Once the client confirms the order and pays the first milestone, the first half of your fee is released.

  4. 4

    Handover

    The balance half is paid when the project is handed over and the client clears the final payment.

Designs that actually get built

AI can draw a dream home in seconds. Building it takes a skilled team.

Today anyone can make a stunning interior image with AI. But those images don't account for beams and columns, plumbing lines, real material sizes, site conditions or the client's budget, so many of them simply can't be built.

That's where we come in. Our architects plan every detail to the millimetre, and our skilled execution team of carpenters, electricians, painters and site supervisors turns the design into a finished home that looks like the drawing.

Programme terms

    
    
      
  1. The fee is based on the final signed contract value excluding GST, and excluding items the client buys directly (appliances, loose furniture, etc.). If the scope changes, the fee follows the final billed value.
    2. 
      
  2. Payment in two parts: 50% within 7 days of the client's first milestone payment, and 50% within 7 days of handover and final payment.
    3. 
      
  3. A lead is registered to the first partner who shares the client's name and number. Registration is valid for 90 days from the date shared. If the client is already in touch with us, we'll tell you within 48 hours.
    4. 
      
  4. No fee is payable if the client doesn't sign, or cancels before the first milestone payment.
    5. 
      
  5. Projects under ₹10 lakh are outside the programme, and no referral fee is paid on them. For projects above ₹50 lakh, the fee is 3% of project value, capped at ₹2.5 lakh.

  6. Planning & visualisation-only projects earn 10% of the design fee (minimum ₹5,000), paid within 7 days of the client's design-fee payment. If the client later hires us to execute, the slab fee is paid as well.
    7. 
      
  7. Fees are paid by bank transfer, with TDS deducted as required by law.
    8. 
      
  8. Terms may be updated with 30 days' notice. Leads already registered are always paid on the terms in force when they were shared.
